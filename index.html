This policy describes how Drone Blast! handles information.

Game data on your device

The game saves high scores, progress towards achievements, settings, appearance preferences and Premium entitlement information on your device so these features work between sessions.

Leaderboards and achievements

When you connect a supported gaming service, the game can submit high scores and achievement updates to that provider. The game can also display leaderboard entries, which may include player names, ranks and scores. Visibility depends on your profile and privacy settings with that service.

Advertising and privacy choices

In builds with advertising enabled, Google AdMob may process information such as IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Google’s consent tools may present choices based on your region and settings. Where the game displays Privacy Choices, use it to review available options. You can also manage advertising preferences through your device and Google account settings.

Purchases

Your platform’s purchase service processes Premium purchases. The game receives information such as the product, purchase status and a transaction record or token to verify and restore access. You manage payment information through the service that processed the purchase.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact support, your email address and the information you include are used to respond and investigate the issue. You can use the same contact to ask about access, correction or deletion of information handled by PerryR Games.

Proposed privacy contact, shared with the Sub Attack! policy: app.support.pjconsulting@gmail.com.

Retention and deletion

Local game data generally remains until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it, subject to device backup and restore. To manage scores, achievements or purchase records held by an external service, use that provider’s account and privacy controls.
